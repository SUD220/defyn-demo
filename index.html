About Us

Hack4r is a cybersecurity services and learning company focused on protecting digital ecosystems with practical, responsible security work. We support organizations with VAPT, security consulting, incident guidance, and awareness programs that reduce real-world risk.

Alongside our services, we publish cybersecurity blog content for students, beginners, and professionals who want to build strong technical foundations. Our mission is simple: secure businesses, train people, and grow a responsible security community.
